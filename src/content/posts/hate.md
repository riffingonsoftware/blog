I've told more than one boss over the years, "If I never piss anyone off, I'm probably not doing my job." Why? Too many people are afraid to make decisions. When you make decisions, you will sometimes go against what someone wants. I'm not talking about the easy decisions. I'm talking about the ones wherein no one can possibly know, with certainty, what the right answer is.

So, let's back up a bit. I'm not talking about just being an asshole. Plenty of people are assholes. That's not special. It's probably not something you should strive to emulate.
