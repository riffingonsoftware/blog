A little while back, I had the great pleasure of serving as a director of engineering, my first role as a people manager. I was not hands-on on a regular basis, but I was responsible for the technical outcomes of my teams. I would provide direction, allow them to make a great many decisions, and sometimes redirect. My teams got things done. (Thank you, teams!)

Guess what. It was always vibe coding. I got the very best agents I could, then flesh and blood humans, provided direction, sometimes correction, and adjusted outcomes as needed. Then people, today LLMs and agent harnesses.
