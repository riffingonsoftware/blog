I like to say all code is technical debt, but most engineers I know view technical debt strictly as a bad thing. Business leaders, well technical business leaders, will likely agree technical debt is a way to become and remain a successful business. Maybe, just maybe, it can be even more than that.

I would hazard a guess most engineers who have worked in a legacy code base would do things very differently given the chance to rewrite from scratch.
